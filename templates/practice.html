How to use past contests

  1. Time yourself. Each individual round is 3 questions in 10 minutes; the team round is 6 questions in 15 minutes.
  2. Do the rounds you will be entered in. At a meet you take three of the five individual rounds; ask your coach which ones.
  3. Check, then redo. Work through anything you missed, then try the same round from another season.
