How to join the league

There is no online registration. A school joins IMLEM by contacting the league president.

Steps

  1. Read the Bylaws. They describe team make-up, the test, meet procedures and each school’s duties.
  2. Contact the league president about joining, league policies, which cluster you would be in, and hosting a meet.
  3. Ask about dues. Dues and reimbursements go to the league treasurer (see who to contact).
